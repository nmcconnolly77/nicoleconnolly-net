Poetry

Assorted Verses

A mix of structured and free verse that draws inspiration from nature and love.

The Word spoke

Jaws devoid of love encircling to rend its pound of flesh fiber by fiber from the one entitled to it Rags and dirt the highest honor that a world reigned by the fetid would bestow upon one whose singing could bring sunsets and stars to tears of joy Write in blood the name of every small droplet of being that fills up the gulf carved into the heart of the Earth with your own Sing again with us in unison holding hands awash in light pure

Fruits

Raspberry essence tart and fresh On display with that of tangerines, Yearning stirred by the view of pineapples and Glory of limes their fullness. Blueberries piled on generously In the midst of ripe plums, Vista before me completed by blackberries their radiance upon a dry ground no longer to be soaked through with tears

Worship

Ground shaking, flash cracking tons above let loose barraging inhabitants small Wave from refreshing wet soil rain returning to the air frogs humbly singing thanks Roar of ecstatic voices rising from the Amazon every tree hosting a choir Updraft raising to glory over every crashing wave wings born to soar the sunsets Hums hovering over soft yellow on the face of the meadowside gentle touch upon every flower Pines giving way under licking heat crumbling to ashen piles that give birth to skyscrapers Every color shining from the prism of Your handiwork carries images of the Face I love

Do

. Called to the cliff's edge Fall or fly Will I die to my way of under standing what to do and how to listen to you I choose...

february

ripples on the water as petals kiss the river where we lay under the pendula rosea "i'll be your spring," you say as the chill hits my ears, the rest of me toasty under the jacket marked with your name during the time when trees expect to die, your love is enough to keep me alive

The sun rises in the east

Ray of light unfiltered and pure upon the ear illuminates inner recesses dusted with mold stirring alive to dance on the clouds Perfect eyes welling with tears they don't deserve to cry beholding the knife that stabs the back How should one who outshines the birds in song respond to a violation of nature? Crucible of fire reveals the gold and fires of love outburn it In the ashes of forgotten sin his body is laid to sprout lily of the valley and the light of morning caresses the rose bouquet he hands his beloved again

Hurricane

Palace on the sand collapses under onslaughts of Rainfall driven hard by winds of adversity Palm trees swaying under the weight dash their Milk against the concrete walls Seashell adorned roofs sparkle in the setting sun like many a Jewel

Cherry

Purple drops as dew alight upon skin despite extra care taken Mar and flaw soak within Sweet at first then bitter Stubborn stain digs in deeper until at once it decides to relinquish its grasp upon the heart and leave it cleaner than before

Bow

Centered in the sky Aligned with your eye Droplets falling high Together refract light Mathematics being the hues of watercolor paint that the artist dips his sable hair brush into Physics the flow of his hand Neurology the canvas And the magic of subjective experience to converge these into a cohesive scene with only your shadow in the center Encircled by every type of visible light arranged in perfect order is the perfect focal point A collective promise recited one by one to recipients man and beast laid before their eyes to see proof of the reprieve

Sensory

White on cream, such things unseen one cannot dream No longer agleam Boredom bursts at seams, begging for release, please, from the unease of the disease of monotony
